Acquaintance Party 2026

Announcement: Acquaintance Party Payment

Good day, Lewisians! Please be informed that the Acquaintance Party 2026 will be held on October 9, 2026, from 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM at the Sorsogon Convention Center, with the theme “Black Tie / GALA / Formal.” 💳 Fee: ₱385.00 📍 Where to Pay: TLC Book Store 📅 Start of Payment: September 22, 2026 📅 Deadline of Payment: October 2, 2026 • First Year Students – Attendance and payment are required. Students with a valid reason for not attending are still required to pay the fee. • 2nd to 4th Year Students – Attendance is optional. Only students who wish to attend are required to pay the ₱385.00 fee. Thank you, Lewisians! See you at the Acquaintance Program!
